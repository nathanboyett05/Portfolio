About Me

I first found my love for coding from a young age when first joining my school's programming UIL team and my school's robotics club. There I gained early experience building and programming projects and learned to enjoy the problem-solving process. That foundation led me to pursue personal development projects, some of which are featured above.

Outside of work, I enjoy chess, reading, archery, and creating 3D maps.
